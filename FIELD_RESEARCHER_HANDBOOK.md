## The 30-Minute Household Survey

### Minute 1-2: Arrival & Greeting

**Say (in household's language):**
```
Tamil:    "Assalam alaikum / Vanakkam. 
          நாங்கள் முஸ்லிம் சமூக பல்கலைக்கழக கல்வி ஆய்வு 
          செய்து கொண்டிருக்கிறோம்..."

English:  "Hello, I'm here to collect information about 
          education and employment in your household. 
          Can I speak with the household head?"

Urdu:     "السلام علیکم۔ میں خاندانی سروے کے لیے 
          معلومات جمع کر رہا ہوں..."
```

**If NO:** "Can I come back at a better time?" (Schedule follow-up)  
**If YES:** Proceed to Consent

---

### Minute 3-4: Consent (Important!)

**Show tablet screen with consent form:**

```
Consent Form (Tamil)

"আমরা মুসলিম সম্প্রদায়ের শিক্ষা এবং চাকরি সম্পর্কে 
গবেষণা করছি। এই তথ্য নীতি তৈরিতে সাহায্য করবে।

আপনার নাম এবং ঠিকানা গোপন থাকবে।
আপনি যেকোনো প্রশ্নের উত্তর না দিতে পারেন।
30 দিনের মধ্যে আপনার তথ্য মুছে ফেলতে বলতে পারেন।

আপনি সম্মত আছেন? হ্যাঁ / না"
```

**Read aloud clearly. Get verbal consent. Record timestamp.**

### Minute 5-30: The AI Survey

**Your job: Listen, facilitate, clarify**

#### How the AI Works:

1. **AI asks a question** (in the household's language)
2. **Respondent answers** (voice or text)
3. **AI validates** ("So you said your son is 25 years old, correct?")
4. **AI skips irrelevant questions** (if no government employee, skips Section F)
5. **AI flags issues** (if something doesn't match earlier data)

#### What You Do:

- **Listen** to the AI's questions
- **Repeat in simpler terms** if respondent doesn't understand
- **Write notes** if anything is confusing (for follow-up later)
- **Point to screen** for respondent to see options
- **Record audio** if respondent gives verbal consent
- **Take photos** of documents (when AI asks)

#### Example Exchange:

```
AI (in Tamil): 
"First, some questions about your family.
What is your name?"

Respondent: "Fatima Begum"

AI: "Fatima, how old are you?"

Respondent: "45"

AI: "How many people live in your household?"

Respondent: "5"

AI: "Are any of them government employees?"

Respondent: "My son. He works at District Municipality."

AI: "That's important. We'll ask more about him.
    Did he use 3.5% Muslim quota for college?"

Respondent: "Yes, he told me that."

AI: [Flags: "Quota used - follow up: ask for certificate/admission letter"]
```

---

### Minute 25-28: Document Capture

**When AI asks: "Do you have any certificates we can photograph?"**

1. **Ask respondent for:**
   - Education certificates (10th, 12th, college degrees)
   - Government job appointment letter
   - Salary slip
   - Ration card
   - Any proof of 3.5% quota use

2. **Photograph with tablet:**
   - Focus clearly
   - Full page visible
   - Good lighting
   - Straight angle

3. **What happens:**
   - System reads text from photo (OCR)
   - Extracts key info (name, date, degree, salary)
   - Scores how clear/readable it is
   - Flags for verification if unclear

---

### Minute 28-30: Quality Check & Thanks

**AI summarizes:**
```
"Let me confirm what you told me:
- Household: 5 people (Fatima, husband, 3 children)
- Your son: degree from [college], 
  government job since [year], salary ₹30,000/month
- He used 3.5% quota for college admission
- Everything correct?"
```

**If respondent says YES:**
- "Thank you very much. Your information will help the government and our community."

**If respondent says NO:**
- Go back and correct. AI will re-ask those sections.

**Give them:**
- Community info sheet (Tamil + English)
- Your phone number (for follow-up if needed)

## Voice Input Tips

### What Works:
- ✅ Speak clearly (normal pace)
- ✅ One sentence at a time
- ✅ Allow 2-3 second pauses
- ✅ Say "repeat" if AI misunderstands

### What Doesn't Work:
- ❌ Mumbling or very fast
- ❌ Too much background noise
- ❌ Overlapping speech (respondent + other family)
- ❌ Truncated answers ("Yeah" → always clarify)

### If Voice Input Fails:
1. Try again (tap microphone again)
2. Use text input instead (respondent types or you type)
3. AI will ask for confirmation

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| AI asks irrelevant question | Skip it (voice: "not applicable") |
| Respondent gives vague answer | AI will ask for clarification |
| Can't hear the AI clearly | Tap speaker icon to repeat |
| Language switch needed | Pause survey, change language, resume |
| No internet? | Survey works offline; syncs when you return |
| Tablet runs out of battery | Connect power bank; data is saved |
| Respondent refuses to answer | Skip question, continue survey |

## At-Home Validation Checks

**Before you leave household, scan for:**

1. **Age logic:** "Respondent is 40, but says daughter is 45" ❌ Go back & correct
2. **Count logic:** "Says 4 members, but only 3 named" ❌ Ask for the 4th person
3. **Income logic:** "Government employee with ₹5,000/month salary" ⚠️ Possible, but ask for confirmation
4. **Education consistency:** "College degree but no school mention" ⚠️ Ask how they got to college

**Don't leave unless:**
- Respondent confirms all data is correct
- Major inconsistencies are resolved
- Documents are photographed (if available)

## After You Leave (At Base)

### Evening Check (15 min)

1. **Connect to WiFi**
2. **Tap "Sync"** on tablet
   - All surveys upload to cloud
   - Photos upload for OCR processing
   - System shows upload status

3. **Review supervisor feedback** (if any)
   - Quality Agent may flag issues
   - You may need to phone respondent for clarification

### Next Day (If Follow-Up Needed)

- Supervisor tells you which households need revisit
- You call respondent: "Hi, just a quick question about..."
- Update survey on tablet
- Re-sync

## Safety & Professionalism

### Be Respectful:
- ✅ Knock / call before entering
- ✅ Ask permission to take photos
- ✅ Never push respondent to answer
- ✅ Respect prayer times (5 times/day in Muslim households)
- ✅ Don't comment on personal details

### Be Professional:
- ✅ Wear ID badge
- ✅ Carry supervisor's contact info
- ✅ Introduce yourself clearly
- ✅ Offer to answer questions
- ✅ Be transparent: "This is for research, not for government surveillance"

### Watch Out For:
- ❌ Don't share household data with neighbors
- ❌ Don't take photos without permission
- ❌ Don't pressure anyone to participate
- ❌ Don't discuss salary/income with third parties

---

## Dealing with Sensitive Topics

### When asked: "Is this for government benefits?"

**Say:** "No, this is independent research. The data helps us understand how well the 3.5% quota is working. Government won't use it to deny anything."

### When asked: "Is this confidential?"

**Say:** "Yes. Your name and address stay private. We only share numbers and statistics, not names. You can ask us to delete your data anytime within 30 days."

### When respondent hesitates:

**Say:** "It's completely up to you. If you're not comfortable, we understand. No pressure."

## Key Reminders

1. **You are the bridge** between AI system and respondent
   - AI is smart but needs your empathy
   - Explain things in simple language
   - Flag anything confusing

2. **Data quality beats speed**
   - Better to spend 40 min with one household than 10 min with two
   - Correct errors on-site (don't leave inconsistencies)

3. **Respect respondents**
   - They're doing us a favor
   - Thank them genuinely
   - Leave them feeling good about participating

4. **Stay safe**
   - Always tell someone where you're going
   - Carry supervisor's phone number
   - Trust your gut

5. **Sync regularly**
   - Every evening if possible
   - Never let data pile up (risk of loss)
   - Synced data = backed up data
